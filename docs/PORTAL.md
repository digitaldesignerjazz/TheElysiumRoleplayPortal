# Portal Protocol

The Elysium Roleplay Portal is Layer 4 of the Elysium stack: the human door.

## Purpose

Give seekers a public, mythic, technically honest place to enter the ecosystem without exposing private runtime.

## How a scene is opened

1. Issue title: `Scene: <realm> — <name>`
2. First comment states realm, present agents, and tone.
3. Play proceeds in comments or in `scenes/<slug>.md` via pull request.
4. Close the issue when the scene is sealed. Link the saga file.

## How a character is claimed

Issue title: `Character: <name>, <title>`

Include:
- chosen title
- house or none
- realm of first arrival
- optional swarm bond (Elara / Lyra / Lumia / Xen / Aura)

Do not include keys, setup-tokens, home addresses, or private family facts.
